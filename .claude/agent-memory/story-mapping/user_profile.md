Repo owner `joerivanarkel`, working solo on RecipeManagement (see [[project-recipe-vision]]).

**Communication style:** Extremely terse. Answers multiple-choice questions with bare letters ("b, c and d") or short phrases ("both", "all of these"). No elaboration unless pushed, and pushing yields little.

**How to apply:**
- Always offer concrete labelled options (A/B/C/D) rather than open-ended questions — they engage with lists, not prose prompts.
- Don't ask "can you say more about that?" — instead propose a specific interpretation and ask them to confirm or reject it.
- Keep your own messages short. They are not reading paragraphs.
- Match their appetite: when offered a menu they tend to pick everything, so expect generous scope rather than a lean MVP.
