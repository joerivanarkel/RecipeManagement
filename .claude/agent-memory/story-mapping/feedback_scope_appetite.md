When offered a menu of optional features, this user consistently chooses **all of them** ("all of these", "no use all of these", "both"). Don't counter-propose a trimmed MVP.

**Why:** Across the story-mapping interview they were offered three separate menus (import methods, cook-mode features, pantry vs. cook-log) and took every option each time. They want the impressive version of the app, not a skeleton. They *did* cut decisively when something genuinely didn't fit their life — they dropped Sunday meal planning without hesitation — so the maximalism is appetite, not indecision.

**How to apply:** Propose generous feature sets and let them cut. Keep ambition at the story-map level but keep each individual story small enough to build in a day — that's where the discipline belongs, not in dropping features. If scope ever needs to shrink, ask which stories to *defer*, not which to delete.
